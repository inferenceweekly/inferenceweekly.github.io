# #1: This week in inference

## :material-book-open-variant: Read

- **[Diagnose Distributed AI Workload Failures with NVIDIA Attribution](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/diagnose-distributed-ai-workload-failures-with-nvidia-attribution/)**, *NVIDIA Technical Blog*. An error analysis engine for distributed AI workloads, and it's great to see NVIDIA open-sourcing a tool their own AI infra team already runs in production.
- **[Progress on provable inference for large language models](https://www.anthropic.com/research/provable-inference)**, *Anthropic*. You can now prove that the output came from the model you asked for, not a cheaper one swapped in by the provider. It's still a research prototype, with proving about 70-95x the cost of normal inference, but it's an interesting step toward verifiable inference.
- **[Designing distributed AI inference: Core concepts and scaling dimensions | Red Hat Developer](https://developers.redhat.com/articles/2026/06/22/designing-distributed-ai-inference-core-concepts-and-scaling-dimensions)**, *developers.redhat.com*. A good primer to read before you write any serving config. It explains why prefill and decode are really two different workloads, and how that drives the choices on disaggregation, parallelism and KV cache.
- **[Open-Source Low-Overhead NVIDIA CUDA PC Sampling | Polar Signals](https://www.polarsignals.com/blog/posts/2026/06/10/nvidia-cuda-pc-sampling)**, *polarsignals.com*. Continuous, instruction-level GPU profiling in production is now open source. You can see which kernels stall and why.
- **[Your GPU Dashboard Is Lying to You](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/your-gpu-dashboard-lying-you-shekhar-patil-gftuf/)**, *linkedin.com*. The utilization number doesn't tell you whether your GPU is doing useful work, because decode is memory-bound and can show 100% while barely computing. Worth a read if you still size your inference fleet from that graph.

## :material-youtube: Watch

- **[What's New in Inference Engineering — Philip Kiely, Baseten](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=75ckHC2LU_0)**, *AI Engineer*. Phil Kiely talks about how many new things came out in inference engineering after he released his book.
- **[Why Inference Matters 12x More Than Training | YC Paper Club](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mHNKuH-9OxU&t=44s)**, *Y Combinator*. A long YC Paper Club session on why inference now matters more than training. It's split into four talks: the lifecycle of an inference request, why not all open-model tokens are the same, compiling models with search instead of heuristics, and SGLang's memory design for agents.
- **[What Is LLM Inference? How AI Models Generate Responses](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=615-yWyS1n0)**, *IBM Technology*. IBM put out a primer on how LLM inference works end to end. It covers tokenization, GPU batching, and the prefill and decode phases, so it's a good one to send to anyone new to the topic.
