# #0: Hello, inference

Welcome to **Inference Weekly**: a short, hand-curated digest for engineers who
serve models in production.

<!-- more -->

Training gets the headlines. Inference is where the GPUs, the latency budgets
and the pager live, and there's no single, simple place that collects the
week's best material on it. This newsletter is that place.

## What to expect

Every Monday you'll get:

- **Read**: articles, engineering blogs and papers
- **Watch**: talks and deep dives
- **Repos**: new projects and notable releases
