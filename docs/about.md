# About

**Inference Weekly** is a free, weekly newsletter for engineers who run models in
production. Training gets the headlines; inference is where the GPUs, the latency
budgets and the pager live. This newsletter collects the week's most useful
material on that side of the stack.

## Scope

- **Serving engines & runtimes**: vLLM, SGLang, TensorRT-LLM, llama.cpp, Dynamo, llm-d
- **Optimization**: batching, KV cache management, quantization, speculative decoding, kernels
- **Infrastructure**: GPUs and accelerators, Kubernetes, autoscaling, disaggregated prefill/decode
- **Operations**: observability, SLOs, capacity planning, cost per token, incidents
- **Economics**: build vs. buy, hardware lifecycles, pricing

## Format

One issue every Monday, published here and sent by email through
[Buttondown](https://buttondown.com/inferenceweekly). Every issue is also
available via [RSS](https://inferenceweekly.github.io/feed_rss_created.xml).
